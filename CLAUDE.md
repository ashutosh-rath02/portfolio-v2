# Portfolio

Next.js 14 app router site with Tailwind and velite MDX content.

## Reading notes

Pages under `/notes` are static HTML in `public/notes` with their own design system, separate from Tailwind. Before adding or editing a chapter, read `docs/notes-design-system.md` and follow it: shared `notes.css` and `notes.js`, book data in `public/notes/assets/library.js`, chapter content in `public/notes/<book>/ch<n>/`. Keep writing plain and professional, with no emojis and no em dashes, and paraphrase the books instead of copying them.
